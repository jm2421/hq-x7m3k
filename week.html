Each layer also carries an edge, so the grid survives a greyscale print and a colour-blind reader: lifting a thick solid edge, cardio a double edge, recovery & movement a dotted edge. A chip that also carries a dotted line across its top, and the words “• if”, is conditional — it happens only when the condition named on the chip holds. It keeps its own layer’s left edge.

Lifting is fixed at three sessions: two at home and one at Discover Strength. Which day is which is a choice, not a fact — it is set in the schedule file, and changing it there moves the calendar, the fitness page and the run sheets together. Discover Strength takes the middle slot.

The two home sessions sit 5 days apart one way round and 2 the other. 2 days is the binding number, not 5 — it is the turnaround the heavier day has to recover inside.
