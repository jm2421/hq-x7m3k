Rules that travel with you

  • Loads are by feel: stop every set two reps short of failure. A strange gym is not the place to test a max.
  • Shoulder: dips depth-capped at upper arm parallel, and skipped any week the left shoulder feels loose. No overhead barbell pressing; landmine or single-arm dumbbell only, and only when the neck is quiet.
  • Low back: hinge with a hex bar, dumbbells or a kettlebell, never a conventional barbell deadlift from the floor. Rows with the ribs over the pelvis, no rocking.
  • Neck: the neck block and chin tucks come first on any day. A stiff neck drops the lateral raises and any pressing that makes the head crane.
  • Recovery score amber: drop the last block. Red: walk instead.
  • Two home sessions a week is the plan's floor; on a trip, two sessions of this kind keep the week whole.

The pool, the slots and the time model live in travel.json; the movements carry the same cautions as the home catalogue (low back, neck, deep knee, shoulder). Selections are remembered on this device.
