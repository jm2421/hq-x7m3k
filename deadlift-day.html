Rules

Low back tight before you start? Skip the hex dead entirely — substitute Voltra pull-through 4 × 10. Don't grind a tight back.
Neck stiff or symptomatic? Isometric holds and chin tucks only — 1 min, three directions; the loaded NeckSlevel work comes out, no range work, and skip Barbell bench press, DB lateral raise, Farmer's carry, Hip thrust, Inverted sit-ups, Landmine press, NeckSlevel. It also takes out, on the mobility page: Thoracic openers over the roller on Sunday Swap rather than drop the hinge: Hex bar deadlift → Voltra pull-through.
Low back sore, not just tight? Do the unloaded half of the session only — pulls, presses that are not braced off an arch, and the neck block — skip Barbell bench press, Farmer's carry, Hip thrust, Inverted sit-ups, Landmine press, Med-ball rotational toss, PureTorque anti-rotation hold, PureTorque rotation — Base Torque, Seated row. Swap rather than drop the hinge: Hex bar deadlift → Voltra pull-through.
Knee sore or swollen? Nothing that goes past ninety degrees of knee flexion under load. Where the day has leg extensions, cut the load rather than the range, and skip Leg curl. It also takes out, on the mobility page: Couch stretch on sauna days; Couch stretch + pec holds on Sunday
Hex dead stops at RPE 8. Bench and the rest can go 8–9; the hinge is the one with a ceiling.
Undulate with Monday. Week A: hex dead heavy (5 × 5 at RPE 8); week B: hex dead moderate (same sets at 85%, RPE 6–7). Every anchor gets a heavy exposure every two weeks.
Set the whole block up first — bar loaded, pull-up bar clear, bench set. Transitions 10–20 s; full rest between rounds.
Grinding by round 3? Take 30 extra seconds between rounds.
Whoop yellow: cut Block D first — that puts the day at 75 min.
Every 4th week is a deload: Deload: Block P 2 rounds, anchors 3 sets at 70% at RPE 6, no Block C or D, one Norway at 80% MAP, no REHIT, maintenance calories. The foot and elbow rehab live in this week.
