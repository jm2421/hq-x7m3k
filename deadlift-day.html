Rules

Low back tight before you start? Skip the hex dead entirely — substitute Voltra pull-through 4 × 10. Don't grind a tight back.
Hex dead stops at RPE 8. Bench and the rest can go 8–9; the hinge is the one with a ceiling.
Set the whole block up first — bar loaded, pull-up bar clear, bench set. Transitions 10–20 s; full rest between rounds.
Grinding by round 3? Take 30 extra seconds between rounds.
Whoop yellow: cut Block D first — that puts the day at 75 min.
