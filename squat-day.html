Rules

Set the whole block up first — belt squat loaded, bar in reach, DBs out. Transitions 10–20 s; full rest between rounds.
Main lifts can chase RPE 8–9. Accessories inside a heavy block never go to failure.
Grinding by round 3? Take 30 extra seconds between rounds. Don't compromise the anchor lift to hit the clock.
Whoop yellow: cut Block D and the finisher first — that puts the day at 63 min.
Equipment competing? Run it as a circuit (all of A1, then A2) and accept ~5 min more.
