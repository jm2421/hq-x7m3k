One honest caveat on the hex dead row: v2 makes high handles the default, which shortens the range, so 315 on high handles is not the same lift as 315 was in v1. Either record the handle height every session from here and treat the next test as a new baseline, or pull one benchmark set from the low handles so the April/June numbers stay comparable. Pick one and be consistent — don't let the number drift upward on a change in the lift.

What changed

v1 → v2 in one paragraph

Fifteen changes from the July 2026 review are now built into the plan rather than sitting in a sidebar: a short med-ball power block opens both home lift days; direct neck work (your NeckSlevel) is on the schedule; deadlift day gained pull-ups and face pulls to fix a pull-poor session; farmer's carries replaced hammer curls; squat-day pressing moved to low-incline DB; the hex dead got an RPE ceiling and high handles by default; knee and ankle work got more specific; the REHIT warm-up got longer and the sprints seated; flexibility moved into the sauna; Sunday mobility got defined; the Pallof press moved onto your PureTorque; the farmer's carry got a real load target; and myo-reps plus an explicit block-priority tier keep the added time manageable. Inverted sit-ups stayed exactly where they were. Everything else — the weekly schedule, the DS/home rotation, cardio targets, loads, tempos, progression logic — is carried over untouched.
